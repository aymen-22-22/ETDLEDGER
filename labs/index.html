Cyber labs

Training environments built for your security team

We design and run isolated lab networks where your team practices defense and response on realistic infrastructure, then reset them to a clean state for the next session.

Plan a lab →See lab types

Inside a lab

A realistic network, completely separated from yours

Each lab mirrors the shape of a real company network: a perimeter, a DMZ, an internal network and monitoring. It runs in isolation, so your team can practice without touching live systems.

  • Designed around your own architecture and tools
  • Separate environments for each team or participant
  • Runs on InfraLedger, on our hardware or on yours

Lab types

Start from a template, or build something custom

Every lab is tailored to your team's level and the skills you want to build.

Perimeter defense

Practice firewall rules, network segmentation and VPN access on a realistic company network.

Web application protection

Configure and tune a web application firewall against realistic traffic, without risk to live services.

Red team vs. blue team

One team works within an agreed scope while the other detects and responds, with a debrief at the end.

Detection engineering

Build and test SIEM detections and dashboards on telemetry generated inside the lab.

Incident response drills

Run a timed scenario from first alert to containment, and review how the team handled it.

Training events

Capture-the-flag style challenges for training days, onboarding or recruiting.

How it works

From first call to a clean reset

  1. 01

    Scope

    We agree on goals, participants, skills to practice and rules of engagement.

  2. 02

    Build

    We build the lab network, services and scenarios to match your environment.

  3. 03

    Run

    Your team connects over VPN. Every action in the lab is logged.

  4. 04

    Reset and report

    The lab is rebuilt to a clean state, and you receive a session report.

Safety

Safe by design

Labs are built for authorized training only, with safeguards at every layer.

Fully isolated

Each lab runs on its own network with no route to the internet or your production systems.

Controlled access

Participants connect with individual VPN credentials that expire when the session ends.

Everything recorded

Activity in the lab is logged, so sessions can be reviewed and debriefed.

Written scope

Every engagement has an agreed scope and rules of engagement before anyone connects.

Clean resets

Labs are rebuilt from a known state between sessions, so every team starts the same.

Torn down after

When the engagement ends, the lab and its data are removed.
