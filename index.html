FAQ

Questions, answered

How is InfraLedger different from Heroku, Render or Railway?

Those platforms run your apps on their cloud. InfraLedger gives you the same self-service workflow on servers you own, from a single machine to a Kubernetes cluster, and records every action in an audit log you control.

What do I need to run it?

A Linux server with Docker and Docker Compose is enough to start. To grow, connect a Kubernetes cluster. We help size the servers for your workload during onboarding.

Do you provide support?

Yes. ETDLedger's engineers help with installation, onboarding and ongoing support, and can operate the platform for you.

Where is my data stored?

On your infrastructure. Applications, databases, logs and the audit log all stay on servers you control. ETDLedger only has access if you grant it.
